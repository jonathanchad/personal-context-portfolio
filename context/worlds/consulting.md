## Standing rule

**Retainers have time caps. Over-servicing is the recurring failure
mode.** Toggl is the measurement; the `client-time-tracker` skill logs to
the Notion **Client Retainers** database. If hours can't be measured
(Toggl unwired), say the number is an estimate — don't present it as fact.

## Rules for agents

  participant domain (Donna's rule). Client shorthand above helps routing.
- Any proposal, SOW or invoice that is drafted but unsent is a
  **priority-one close**, not admin. Surface it.
- Don't invent fee numbers. Use `[FEE]` placeholders where unknown.
